Game
The game is a dungeon crawler where the player is created, random gear is generated from a set of items, and the player can choose what to do in his turn. Every 3 turns there is a possibility to go shopping where the player can use gold gathered from monsters and treasures. Monsters drop treasures when defeated and the player can also choose to use a chance to find treasure or defeat a monster and certainly get a treasure from the defeated monster. Periodically, for example every 5 or 10 turns a stronger monster comes, a boss, that gives better loot, upgrades the player's stats (base damage, hitpoints, giving health potions etc.) when defeated. The game ends when the player is defeated or has defeated 3 bosses. The game is text-based.

Create a player object and his gear
Create the enemies which stats are dependent on the amount of bosses defeated
Create a game loop for progressing through turns and losing the game
Create combat and treasure mechanics

Additional points can include making the shop, changing gear from the shop to the user, scoreboard saved in a file, player leveling etc.
